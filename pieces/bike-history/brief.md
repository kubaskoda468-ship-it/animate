# A short history of the bicycle — brief

- **Spec:** 20.0s, 9:16 1080×1920, 24fps, 120 BPM, music only (score in code). Style: cut paper. Format: F2 morph chain (3 shape morphs, 1 hard cut on the payoff).
- **Idea:** it took 70 years to find the bicycle — and the first bike a child gets today has no pedals, like the 1817 one.
- **Device:** each era closes in on its front wheel, which becomes the next era's wheel; the spark (orange, the rider) gains a ray per era (5 → 10).
